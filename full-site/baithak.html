बैठक / Baithak

A room small enough that nobody is only an audience.

The sitting

Once a month the laboratory opens its circle. A Baithak is not a performance — it is a sitting: musicians try a new composition, an actor reads a scene still in rehearsal, a painter shows what the last gathering left on their canvas, and everyone in the room is close enough to answer back.

There is no stage and no green room. Work-in-progress is shown unfinished on purpose, because the room's response is part of the making. People of every age sit together — this has been the collective's way of working for three decades.

Baithaks are small, and they move — homes, studios, courtyards. Dates are announced on Instagram.

Bring a बैठक to your space.

Our sittings live in rooms that belong to people, not institutions. If you can open yours, we will fill it.

What you need

  • A room for a small circle of people — a living room, a studio, a terrace, a courtyard
  • Floor seating, mostly; chairs for those who need them
  • A space accessible to everyone

What we bring

  • Musicians, and songs from the Ruhdaari repertoire
  • Readings and work-in-progress from the laboratory
  • The conversation that follows, and the chai that carries it

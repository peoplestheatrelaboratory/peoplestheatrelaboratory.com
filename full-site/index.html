Peoples Theatre Laboratory

Three decades of Total Theatre in Vadodara — gatherings of song, paint and stage.

Who we are

Peoples Theatre Laboratory is an experimental theatre collective in Vadodara, working in the spirit of Total Theatre — where painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians and people from social practice build a production together, and no single form is allowed to hold the room alone.

We have been staging plays, running residential workshops and experimenting with people of every age for three decades.

Collaborations

No single form is allowed to hold the room alone.

How we work

Collaboration is not a department here — it is the method. Every PTL production is built between forms: painters work onstage at Ruhdaari while the musicians play; sculptors and art historians shape the workshops; people from social practice keep the laboratory honest about who theatre is for.

The gatherings draw people across generations, and the room is used to welcoming an artist it has never met before.

Who we look for

Artists
Any form

Singers, players, painters, poets, dancers, makers of any kind — bring your practice into a room that will not leave it unchanged.

Venues
Rooms

Clubs, art centres, courtyards, homes. Ruhdaari has filled MilanKunj Club in Vadodara and the Kanoria Centre for Arts in Ahmedabad.

Organisations
Fellow travellers

Schools, collectives and institutions who want the workshop, the sitting or the gathering to reach their own people.

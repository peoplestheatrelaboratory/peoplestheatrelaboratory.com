The collective

Never a cast
and a crew

Peoples Theatre Laboratory has never been a cast and a crew. It works in the spirit of Total Theatre: the painter sits in the rehearsal, the musician argues inside the workshop, the art historian questions the director — and the production belongs to all of them at once.

Three decades in Vadodara. Works like टोबा टेक सिंह and other experiments that were allowed to fail in front of each other, which is the only way a laboratory stays a laboratory.

No single form is allowed to hold the room alone. If you carry a form we haven't met yet — bring it.

Spike mark: your room

Host a Baithak

A courtyard, a studio, a living room with the furniture pushed back — if twenty people can sit close enough to answer back, it can hold a Baithak. We bring the songs and the work-in-progress; you bring the room.

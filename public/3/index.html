लोगThe People

the best introduction is a baithak — come meet everyone mid-song

The collective

Not a cast and a crew

Painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians and people from social practice — the painter is in the rehearsal, the musician is in the workshop, the art historian argues with the director, and the production belongs to all of them at once.

Three decades

The spirit of Total Theatre

Plays, residential workshops, and experiments with people of every age in Vadodara — no single form allowed to hold the room alone.

A scrap from the room

"I came for the songs of Kabir and left having painted for the first time."

— a first-time painter

An invitation

Carry a form we haven't met

Singers, players, painters, poets, dancers, makers of any kind — bring your practice into a room that will not leave it unchanged.

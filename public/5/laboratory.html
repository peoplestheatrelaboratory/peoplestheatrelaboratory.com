A laboratory,
not a company.

For thirty years in Vadodara we have worked in the idiom of total theatre — in the lineage of Grotowski — where nothing is decoration, and no single artform is allowed to win.

Painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians, people from social practice. सब साथ में, एक ही कमरे में।

We hold residential workshops. We work with every age. While the room sings, painters paint — and what is painted stays with the laboratory. It is not for sale.

Work that has passed through this floor: टोबा टेक सिंह · उल्टी वा · Sharad Poonam · Baithak, month after month · Kabir Sang Ruhdaari, moon after moon.

from the songbook — one page per song, like a liner note.

झीनी झीनी बीनी चदरिया

Kabir · as sung at Ruhdaari recording to come

Kabir was a weaver of Kashi. He looked at the cloth on his own loom and saw a body being woven — thread by thread, breath by breath. This is the song of that shawl.

झीनी झीनी बीनी चदरिया ॥

Fine, so fine — this woven shawl.

काहे कै ताना काहे कै भरनी,
कौन तार से बीनी चदरिया ॥

Of what is the warp, of what the weft? From which thread was this shawl woven?

इंगला पिंगला ताना भरनी,
सुखमन तार से बीनी चदरिया ॥

Ingla and Pingla are the warp and the weft; from the Sushumna thread the shawl is woven.

आठ कँवल दल चरखा डोलै,
पाँच तत्त गुन तीनी चदरिया ॥

The spinning-wheel of the eight-petalled lotus turns; five elements, three qualities — such is the shawl.

साँ को सियत मास दस लागे,
ठोंक ठोंक कै बीनी चदरिया ॥

Ten months it took the Maker to stitch it, beating the weave tight, thread on thread.

सो चादर सुर नर मुनि ओढ़ी,
ओढ़ि कै मैली कीनी चदरिया ॥

Gods, humans, sages wrapped themselves in that shawl — wore it, and soiled it.

दास कबीर जतन करि ओढ़ी,
ज्यों की त्यों धरि दीनी चदरिया ॥

Kabir, the servant, wore it with such care — and laid it down just as it was given.

चदरिया — the shawl is the body. The song is sung at partings and at births, which Kabir insists are the same doorway.

इंगला · पिंगला · सुखमन — the three subtle channels of yogic breath. Kabir strings the loom with anatomy.

मास दस — ten lunar months at the loom: the time a body takes to be woven in the womb.

At the baithak this one begins slow, almost spoken — the refrain returns each time a little louder, until the room carries it alone.

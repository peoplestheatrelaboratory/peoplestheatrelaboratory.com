The collective — never a cast & crew

The painter is in the rehearsal. The musician is in the workshop. The art historian argues with the director — and the production belongs to all of them at once.

Thirty years in Vadodara, working in the spirit of Total Theatre. No single form is allowed to hold the room alone — which is why no two evenings here have ever looked alike.

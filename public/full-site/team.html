The Collective

Painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians — one room.

Three decades

Peoples Theatre Laboratory has never been a cast and a crew. It is a collective that works in the spirit of Total Theatre: the painter is in the rehearsal, the musician is in the workshop, the art historian argues with the director, and the production belongs to all of them at once.

For three decades this room has staged plays, run residential workshops, and experimented with people of every age in Vadodara.

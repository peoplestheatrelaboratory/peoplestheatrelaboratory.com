Who we are

Peoples Theatre Laboratory is an experimental theatre collective in Vadodara, working in the spirit of Total Theatre — where painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians and people from social practice build a production together, and no single form is allowed to hold the room alone.

We have been staging plays, running residential workshops and experimenting with people of every age for three decades.

What we gather for

Every month

बैठक

Baithak

Our monthly sitting. Music, readings and work-in-progress, shared in a room small enough that nobody is only an audience.

कबीर संग रुहदारी

Kabir Sang Ruhdaari

An intimate gathering around the voices of Kabir, Meera, Bulleh Shah, Raidaas and Lalon Fakir — original compositions, folk songs and live painting, held on Jyestha Poornima. Not a cultural presentation. A shared space of reflection. Come as you are. Sing along. Paint. Breathe.

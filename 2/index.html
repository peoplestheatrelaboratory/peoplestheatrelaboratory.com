Act I

The Laboratory

We are an experimental theatre collective working in the spirit of Total Theatre — painters, sculptors, musicians, art historians and people from social practice build every production together, and no single form is allowed to hold the room alone.

Three decades of plays, residential workshops, and experiments with people of every age, in Vadodara.

Act II

The Sitting बैठक

Once a month the laboratory opens its circle. A Baithak is not a performance — it is a sitting: musicians try a new composition, an actor reads a scene still in rehearsal, a painter shows what the last gathering left on their canvas, and everyone in the room is close enough to answer back.

There is no green room. Work-in-progress is shown unfinished on purpose, because the room's response is part of the making. Baithaks are small, and they move — homes, studios, courtyards.

[ Interval. The chai is poured. ]

॥

A बैठक needs only a room.

Perhaps yours — a living room, a studio, a terrace, a courtyard. If you can gather a small circle of people, we bring the songs, the readings, and the conversation that follows.

Act III

The Full Moon कबीर संग रुहदारी

Once a year, on Jyestha Poornima, the laboratory gathers around the voices of Kabir, Meera, Bulleh Shah, Raidaas and Lalon Fakir — original compositions and folk songs, sung in a room that sings back. While the musicians play, painters work beside the stage; the evening leaves behind canvases.

It is not a cultural presentation. It is a shared space of reflection. Contribution-based, by registration alone — no tickets, no rows of passive seats.
